Overview

The crowdstrike_fdr source ingests CrowdStrike Falcon Data Replicator (FDR) data. It reads file notifications from the CrowdStrike-managed SQS queue for your FDR feed and downloads the referenced objects from the CrowdStrike-managed S3 bucket.

Supported pipeline types: logs
